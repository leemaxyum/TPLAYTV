# Fleavo Brand Guide

## Positioning
**Fleavo is the room a college runs on — ready the moment you need it, invisible the moment you don't.**

## Design rules
- Premium, calm, dependable, warm, and fleet only during live activities.
- Matte off-white or soft ink surfaces; terracotta for primary/live status.
- No glass, decorative gradients, public failure states, or guilt-driven gamification.
- Wrong answers are neutral and private by default.
- Leaderboards are optional; personal progress comes before comparison.

## Language
- “Open a room”
- “Join with a code”
- “Getting everyone in…”
- “From the host”
- Never use Stellar Play, Stellar C&H, or legacy `stellarplay:` storage keys.

## Motion
Shell motion is slow and settled. Live activities can be quick but never alarm-like. The loading board uses three softly paced dots and “Opening your room.”
